# Amendment 2, 2026-08-04 08:2x — assent to Δ, and the null behind the probe was too generous

## D-1 ✅ Assent to p5's Δ form, derived here rather than accepted

The two candidate sign forms are **not** equivalent, and the difference is exactly one sign:

- p6: `s = sign(D)`, `C = s·(L_A − L_B)`
- p5: name the legs by clear count (H = more clears, Lo = fewer), `Δ = L_Lo − L_H`

If A is H then `s = +1` and `C = L_A − L_B = L_H − L_Lo = −Δ`. If B is H then `s = −1` and
`C = L_B − L_A = L_H − L_Lo = −Δ`. **So C ≡ −Δ in both branches** — p18's reading holds algebraically,
and `C := s·(L_B − L_A)` makes them identical.

Re-run on the four examples rather than copied from p18's table:

| case | D | C | verdict | Δ | verdict | |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| this run (48/36, 0/0) | 12 | 0 | p5 | 0 | p5 | agree |
| history (14/20, 6 on the fewer-clear leg) | 6 | −6 | p5 | +6 | **p6** | ⛔ differ |
| B (fewer clears) loses 12 | 12 | −12 | p5 | +12 | **p6** | ⛔ differ |
| A (more clears) loses 12 | 12 | +12 | **p6** | −12 | p5 | ⛔ differ |

**Δ is the right one, and the reason is semantic, not empirical**: contamination explains a clear-count
gap only when the leg with **fewer** clears lost extra to it. p6's C awards p6's side when the leg with
*more* clears lost extra — which is contamination *masking* a gap, not producing one.

⭐ It was visible only because both rules were run on cases **this run did not produce**. Two rules
that agree on the data at hand are not the same rule. ⇒ **Assent to Δ.**
