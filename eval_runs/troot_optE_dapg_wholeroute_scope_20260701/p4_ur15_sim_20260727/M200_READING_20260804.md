## 1. Power — stated first

The left-arm rate measured by **this** run, over both conditions: **0 / 90 = 0.00 %**.

⛔ **The floor as literally specified is degenerate here.** With a measured rate of zero the Poisson
null spread is `sqrt(E_A + E_B)` = **exactly 0**, so `|D| ≥ 2·sd` reduces to `12 ≥ 0` and **cannot
fail**. A test that cannot come out differently is not a test — the stone this lane has been handling
for two days — so it is flagged rather than passed quietly.

Conservative alternative, rule of three on 0/90 (**p ≤ 3/90 = 0.0333**), Poisson form as specified:

| | n | E[L] at the bound |
|---|---|---|
| A | 9 | 0.30 |
| B | 81 | 2.70 |

`sd = sqrt(0.30 + 2.70) = ` **1.732**, so **2·sd = 3.46**.

⭐ And a bound that needs no rate at all: **the contamination actually available in both conditions is
zero** (L_A = L_B = 0). Contamination therefore explains **none** of D — not "less than half of it".
