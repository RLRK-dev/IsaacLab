READING -- p4, after the run.  Measurements above; this block is interpretation, and one part of
it CORRECTS a conclusion I published from the crown sweep three hours earlier.

1. ⛔ CORRECTION to the crown-radius reading.  I wrote there that "the crown owns none of the
   left arm's blockage", from a table taken entirely at the built tilt of 45 degrees.  It is true
   AT 45 and false in general: with the crown removed the left arm keeps 2 clear poses at
   (0.220, 30), 3 at (0.220, 20), 1 at (0.280, 20), 2 at (0.340, 20) and 2 at (0.400, 20).  A
   one-row-of-the-grid measurement was reported as a property of the crown.  This is the second
   time today I have generalised from a slice; the first was calling a placement problem a reach
   problem.

2. ⭐ The lever for the left arm is the TILT, not the spread.  Every left-arm non-zero in either
   sheet is at tilt 20 or 30; there is not one at 45, which is what the cell is built at.  The
   spread moves nothing on its own -- 0.220 through 0.400 at tilt 45 is zero all the way across,
   on both sheets.

3. ⭐ The crown and the tilt are not additive, they gate each other.  With the crown removed,
   four of the eight shallow-tilt points open.  With it pinned at 0.110, exactly one does
   (0.340, 20).  So neither "remove the crown" nor "shallow the tilt" is by itself the answer at
   the built spread; the pair is.  That interaction is precisely what a single sheet would have
   hidden, and it is why the confound flag mattered.

4. ⚠ THE INTERLEAVE COLUMN INVERTS THE SPEC COMMENT, with a scope limit that must travel with
   it.  The comment records (0.22, 45) as interleaving at an 88 mm span and (0.40, 20) as
   clearing.  Measured here: (0.220, 45) does NOT interleave (+9.6 crown-none, +26.2 pinned), and
   (0.400, 20) DOES on the crown-none sheet (+0.0, touching) while reading +22.7 clear on the
   pinned one.  ⛔ SCOPE: this is measured at the start poses the solver CHOSE, so it says "the
   arms as placed touch", not "no placement avoids crossing".  A different pose could clear where
   this reads touching.  The reading is therefore evidence about the configuration the cell
   actually adopts, and not a proof about the span itself.

5. ⚠ (0.340, 45) reads -110.3 mm on BOTH sheets -- the arms are through each other by eleven
   centimetres -- and the right arm has zero clear poses there.  Whatever else is chosen, that
   combination is not a candidate.

⛔ Not a verdict and not a recommendation.  Which mounting to take is p5's call and Rs's to
settle; this is the grid p5 asked for, with the one correction it forced on my earlier reading.
