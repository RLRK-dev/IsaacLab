# The 240-draw mounting grid — the reading

**Artifacts.** Table `SPREAD_TILT_SWEEP_TRIES240.txt`; comparison `GRID_24_VS_240_20260802.txt`
(from `compare_24_vs_240.py`); the 24-draw table it is compared against,
`SPREAD_TILT_SWEEP_TRIES24_20260729.txt` (content sha `7e2423ed…62ee6`, the pinned one).

**What was run.** The real driver, once per point, 24 points = crown {removed, pinned 0.110} ×
spread {0.220, 0.280, 0.340, 0.400} × tilt {45, 30, 20}, at `START_TRIES=240`. Seven points
measured 10:30–11:12 were reused from their own logs; the rest re-solved. **22 of 24 measured** —
two crash (§5).

⛔ Not a verdict and not a recommendation. Which mounting to build is p5's call and Rs's to settle.

---

## 1. ⭐ The built cell's zero is real. It survives ten times the sample.

**crown 0.110, spread 0.220, tilt 45** — `sweep_mounting.py`'s own defaults, the crown following as
`YOKE_SPREAD/2`:

| | L solved | L clear | R solved | R clear | arms closest |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 24 draws | 7 | **0** | 17 | 2 | +26.2 mm |
| 240 draws | **58** | **0** | 132 | 19 | +26.2 mm |

The solved count grows eight-fold and the clear count does not move. ⇒ **At the built mounting the
left arm has no clear start pose, and that is a property of the cell, not of the sample.** The
shape of the original argument — solutions exist and grow while clear ones stay at zero, so it is
placement and not reach — is *strengthened* here, not overturned.

## 2. ⛔ But four zeros were the sample, and that puts every 24-draw sweep in doubt

Of 22 comparable points, **16 read L free = 0 at 24 draws and 12 do at 240**. The four that moved:

| point | L clear at 24 | at 240 |
|---|---|---|
| crown none, spread 0.220, tilt 45 | 0 | **3** |
| crown none, spread 0.280, tilt 45 | 0 | **4** |
| crown 0.110, spread 0.220, tilt 20 | 0 | **2** |
| crown 0.110, spread 0.280, tilt 20 | 0 | **5** |

⇒ A zero from 24 draws is not evidence that no clear pose exists. **Every sweep in this
investigation was run at 24 draws**, and each concluded from a column of zeros:

- `GRASP_CENTRE_SWEEP_20260729.txt` — "there is no grasp-pair centre at which the left arm keeps a
  clear pose", the basis of the t43 verdict §3;
- `WORK_ROW_Y_SWEEP_20260729.txt` — "moving the work row buys the left arm nothing";
- `CROWN_RADIUS_SWEEP_20260729.txt`, `CROWN_HEIGHT_SWEEP_20260802.txt`.

⚠ Their zeros are **not refuted** — twelve of sixteen zeros here survived the bigger sample. They
are **unresolved**: each was one draw count away from being able to say which kind of zero it was,
and none of them said so. ⛔ Where a decision rests on one of those tables, the point it rests on
should be re-run at 240 before the decision is taken.

## 3. Four points satisfy all three legs at one witness pair

| crown | spread | tilt | L clear | R clear | arms closest |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 0.280 | 45 | 4 | 8 | +11.9 mm |
| none | 0.400 | 20 | 8 | 34 | +17.2 mm |
| **0.110** | 0.280 | 20 | 5 | 30 | +14.7 mm |
| **0.110** | 0.340 | 20 | 2 | 34 | +15.3 mm |

⭐ **Two of the four have the head on.** So "a head can be present and the arms still start clear"
is now demonstrated, not argued — at those two mountings, neither of which is the built one.

⚠ At both of those points the crown-removed sheet **fails** while the crown-pinned sheet passes.
That is **not** "the crown helps": a fail is not a refutation (§4), the chosen pose pair differs
between the two sheets, and at (0.280, 20) the crown-removed cell has *more* distinct clear left
poses than the crown-pinned one (3 against 2 — `PACKAGE_NOTES_COURT_ITEMS_20260802.md` §b). What
can be said is narrower and still useful: **a witness was found with the head present and none was
found without it at those two points.**

## 4. ⛔ The asymmetry, restated because the table now depends on it

A **PASS is a witness**: a clear left pose, a clear right pose, and that pair separated at the
commanded 88 mm span. A **fail is not a refutation**: the interleave is read at **one** pose pair,
the chosen one, out of N × M. Twelve rows read "interleaving YES" — none of them says the mounting
has no pair that clears.

**The gap column moves with the sample, which is the same fact from the other side.** At
crown none / spread 0.280 / tilt 45 it went +0.0 → **+11.9**; at 0.400/30, +0.0 → **+21.5**; at
0.110/0.340/45, **−110.3** → +0.0. Nothing moved in the cell. The chosen pose pair changed.

### 3a. ⛔ Correction: the spec comment is reproduced, not contradicted

`INTERLEAVE_COLUMN_READING_20260802.md` §3 said the one readable row of **(0.400, 20)** read the
**opposite** of the spec comment. That was a 24-draw row. At 240 draws, on the rows that are
readable (both arms clear):

| the comment says | readable row at 240 | reads |
|---|---|---|
| (0.220, 45) **interleaves** | crown none: L 3, R 14 | **+0.0 — interleaving** ✓ |
| (0.400, 20) **clears** | crown none: L 8, R 34 | **+17.2 mm — clear** ✓ |

⇒ **Both halves reproduce.** The contradiction was a property of a 24-draw pose pair. §3 of that
document is superseded by this section; the rest of it stands.

## 5. ⛔ Two points cannot be measured at all

**spread 0.340, tilt 30 — on BOTH sheets.** The driver segfaults there at 240 draws, deterministic
at 37 s, inside `mj_forward` on an intermediate IK pose
(`SEGFAULT_AT_SPREAD0340_TILT30_20260802.md`, sha `740eb72a…3dff7e7e` @ `62536001cd`).

⭐ It happens with the crown **and** without it ⇒ the crash is not the head; it is that
spread-and-tilt. The fix (kinematics-only inner loop, controlled) is not applied yet — the driver
is the instrument and this grid was mid-run.

⚠ The rows print `-- NOT MEASURED: no interleave line within 2400s --`, which cannot tell a cap
from a crash. That text is wrong about the mechanism and right about the verdict; it is the next
thing to fix.

## 6. The right arm was never the constraint

R clear counts at 240, all 22 measured points sorted: 5, 5, 8, 10, 14, 15, 16, 19, 26, 27, 30, 31,
31, 32, 34, 34, 34, 34, 35, 37, 37, 38 — against 0–4 at 24 draws. Every point has a clear right

where the right arm read **0** at 24 draws (0.340/45, both sheets) read 15 and 10 at 240 ⇒ those
zeros were the sample too.

## 7. Limits

1. **Start pose only.** The arms move afterwards; a mounting clear at the start is not thereby
   clear for the task.
2. **The left arm's path is checked against a stale partner** — `PACKAGE_NOTES_COURT_ITEMS…` §c.
   A PASS says the arms are clear **where they end up**.
3. **240 is a sample too.** Four zeros moved between 24 and 240; nothing here says none would move
   between 240 and 2400. What is established is **existence** at the four PASS points, never a
   count and never an absence.
4. **Two points unmeasured**, so nothing above is a statement about the grid as a whole.
