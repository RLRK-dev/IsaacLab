(b) YOKE_SPREAD x TILT, with the 88 mm interleave read at every point.
    ⛔ TWO SHEETS (p5 -177(3)): every point is run with the crown REMOVED and
    again with it PINNED at 0.110.  CROWN_R is normally derived as YOKE_SPREAD/2,
    so a single sheet would drag the crown along with the spread and could not
    separate 'wider shoulders helped' from 'a fatter head hurt'.
    ⚠ PINNED MEANS THE RADIUS ONLY.  The crown's LENGTH follows the spread by
    definition -- the capsule runs from -spread to +spread -- so a pinned radius
    is not a pinned object: wide spread gives a long thin bar, narrow spread a
    short fat head.  'The crown was held fixed' must not be read as 'the same
    body was present throughout'. (p5 -179(2))
    ⚠ On the pinned sheet the wide end leaves a head thinner relative to the
    spread than the cell's own rule gives -- the cost of isolating the variable,
    not a proposal about how to build one.
    ⚠ The spec comment records (0.22, 45) as interleaving at an 88 mm span and
    (0.40, 20) as clearing.  That pair is re-measured here, not argued, and both
    readouts are taken at every combination so neither can be lifted off a point
    that was measured for the other.
    ⭐ 'gap on' names the poses the interleave was measured at.  When an arm has no

    poses, so the gap on that row is the distance between arms at a configuration
